A club stands on the bonds between its people.

Just as a building stands firm because of its foundation, our club stands strong because of the bonds that bring us together. Everything we do falls under three kinds of work.

Meadows · August 2026

Service

Visits, drives and distributions for people in Thane who need a hand. Children's homes, workers on the road, and causes we choose as a club.

Fellowship

Photoshoots, district ceremonies, meetings that run long because nobody wants to leave. The friendships are the point, not a side effect.

Leadership

Every member runs something. Board meets, event ownership and district roles teach us to lead while we are still young enough to make mistakes.
